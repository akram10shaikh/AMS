NOTE:

The Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) using Exponentially Weighted Moving Averages (EWMA) is a refined approach to monitor training load. EWMA places greater emphasis on recent workloads while still considering historical data, making it more sensitive to daily fluctuations in training.Interpretation of Ranges (commonly cited in literature): Acute (7 days): 𝜆 =0.25 ; Chronic (28 days): 𝜆=0.069

< 0.8 → Undertraining (low risk but potential loss of fitness)

0.8 – 1.3 → Optimal/“Sweet Spot” (balanced load, reduced injury risk)

> 1.5 → Increased injury risk (spike in load)

> 2.0 → Very high risk (sudden overload)
